# Menus

- In the menus (the Scenario screen and its activity setup, Conquest, Saved Games and
  Settings), the text in a dropdown or a text box is centred vertically in its box and
  starts two game pixels in from the box's left border, level with the items of the
  dropdown's open list. The typing cursor and a selection stay on the text.
- In the buy menu, every section (a faction's items under its header, such as COALITION
  or IMPERATUS) is open when the menu first appears. A player can still close and reopen
  one by pressing its header.
- The buy menu's section headers stand on a slightly darker shade of the list's colour
  than its items, so they read as headings and not as things to buy, in every faction's
  skin.
